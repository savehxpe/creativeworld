# Signal Desk Engine

Outworld Creative — Internal Skill  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Purpose

Track where attention, ads, AI, sound, culture, and commerce are moving. Turn signals into content ideas and strategic recommendations for clients.

---

## Inputs

- Daily scan of: Meta Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center, industry newsletters, platform updates, creator economy reports
- Client industries (restaurant, fashion, hospitality, beauty, fintech, events)
- Cultural moments (holidays, sports, music drops, local events)

---

## Process

1. **Morning scan (10 min):** Check sources for anything surprising or counterintuitive
2. **Signal identification:** What trend, platform change, or industry shift matters for clients?
3. **Translation:** Turn the signal into a brand-relevant insight
4. **Content creation:** Draft X post + Instagram carousel
5. **Client alert:** If a signal directly affects a client, send them a brief note
6. **Archive:** Save signal in memory engine for future reference

**Daily output:**
- 1 X post
- 1 Instagram carousel
- 1 saved insight for archive

---

## Output

- X post (draft)
- Instagram carousel (7-slide draft)
- Client alert (if relevant)
- Archived insight with source link

---

## Quality Check

- [ ] Signal is sourced from credible source
- [ ] Translation is relevant to brand/creator audience
- [ ] Content adds value, not just reposts news
- [ ] Client alerts are brief and actionable
- [ ] Archive is searchable and tagged

---

## Example Use

**Signal:** TikTok announces new ad format that rewards first 3 seconds more heavily
**Translation:** "Brands with weak hooks will get buried even faster. Those with strong 5-second ads will win cheaper CPMs."
**X post:**
```
TikTok just changed the algorithm again.

First 3 seconds now matter more than ever.

Brands with weak hooks will get buried.
Brands with strong hooks will win cheaper attention.

The gap between good creative and bad creative
is about to become a canyon.

Request a Creative Diagnosis.
```
**Client alert:** Sent to all current clients: "Update your hooks. Platform just raised the bar."
