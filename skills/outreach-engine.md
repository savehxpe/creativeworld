# Outreach Engine

Outworld Creative — Internal Skill  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Purpose

Write DMs, emails, follow-ups, and call openers that get replies. Not sales pitches — curiosity-driven messages that offer value first.

---

## Inputs

- Target brand name and handle
- Niche
- Current content quality (weak, decent, strong)
- Specific observation about their brand
- Channel (Instagram DM, LinkedIn, cold email)

---

## Process

1. **Research (2 min):** Check their last 12 posts, bio, website, competitor landscape
2. **Find the gap:** What's one specific thing they're missing?
3. **Write the hook:** First line must show you've actually looked at their brand
4. **Offer value:** "I created something for your brand" — not "I want to sell you something"
5. **Make it easy:** One-click yes. No forms, no calls, no commitment.
6. **Follow-up cadence:** Day 3 (gentle reminder), Day 7 (last follow-up), Day 14 (soft close)

**Message rules:**
- Max 4 short paragraphs
- No prices
- No packages
- No "we're the best"
- Specific observation beats generic compliment
- Question beats statement

---

## Output

- Opening message (customized to brand)
- Day 3 follow-up
- Day 7 follow-up
- Day 14 soft close
- Spec ad send message (after they say yes)

---

## Quality Check

- [ ] Message shows real research, not copy-paste
- [ ] Specific observation is mentioned in first 2 lines
- [ ] No prices, packages, or sales language
- [ ] CTA is easy ("Mind if I send it?" not "Book a call")
- [ ] Follow-ups feel polite, not desperate

---

## Example Use

**Brand:** Restaurant with 20K followers, weak hooks, no booking link
**Opening DM:**
```
Hey [Name], I'm Tshepo from Outworld Creative.

I came across your restaurant and noticed something: your food looks incredible but your content doesn't show the experience. People don't just book tables for food — they book for the feeling.

I created a spec ad concept showing how a restaurant like yours could turn profile views into actual reservations. Not a pitch — just a preview.

Mind if I send it through?

Tshepo
Outworld Creative
```

**Day 3 follow-up:**
```
Hey [Name], quick follow-up. No pressure — just wanted to make sure it didn't get lost in the DMs.

If you're open to seeing how your restaurant could look with stronger campaign creative, I'm happy to send it over.

Tshepo
```
