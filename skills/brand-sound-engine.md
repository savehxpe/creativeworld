# Brand Sound Engine

Outworld Creative — Internal Skill  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Purpose

Create short, memorable sounds that make brands recognizable before the logo appears. 5-second tags, 15-second jingles, product reveal sounds, and campaign sound beds.

---

## Inputs

- Brand name and personality
- Niche (restaurant, fashion, hospitality, etc.)
- Emotional direction (confident, calm, energetic, luxurious)
- Platform (Reels, TikTok, Stories, TV)
- Reference sounds (if any)

---

## Process

1. **Analyze the brand's visual identity:** What does the brand *look* like? Translate that into sonic language.
2. **Define the sonic signature:** 1-3 notes or a rhythm that becomes the brand's audio fingerprint.
3. **Create the 5-second tag:** The shortest version — the "hello" of the brand.
4. **Extend to 15-second jingle:** Build a mini-composition around the tag.
5. **Design reveal sounds:** Sonic punctuation for product launches, drops, or announcements.
6. **Build the campaign bed:** Background music that ties multiple ads together sonically.

**Per-niche sound direction:**
- Restaurant: Warm, appetizing, social ambience
- Fashion: Confident, trendy, bass-forward
- Hospitality: Escape, peace, nature-infused
- Beauty: Soft, trust-building, gentle textures
- Events: High energy, FOMO, bass drops
- Fintech: Clean, modern, trust signals

---

## Output

- 5-second brand tag (WAV + MP3)
- 15-second ad jingle (WAV + MP3)
- Product reveal sound (WAV + MP3)
- Campaign sound bed (WAV + MP3)
- Sonic brand guidelines (PDF)

---

## Quality Check

- [ ] Sound is recognizable in 1 second
- [ ] Works with and without visuals
- [ ] Feels native to the brand, not generic stock music
- [ ] Multiple lengths for different platforms
- [ ] Consistent across all brand touchpoints

---

## Example Use

**Brand:** Luxury restaurant in Sandton
**Personality:** Exclusive, warm, intimate
**Sonic signature:** Warm bass note + soft wine glass harmonic
**5-sec tag:** Bass + glass harmonic + silence
**15-sec jingle:** Slow build of kitchen ambience → bass entrance → glass harmonic → silence
**Reveal sound:** Sizzle + bass sting + "Only 3 tables left" voice
**Campaign bed:** 30-second loop of warm ambience + subtle bass pulse
