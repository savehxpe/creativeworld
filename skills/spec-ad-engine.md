# Spec Ad Engine

Outworld Creative — Internal Skill  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Purpose

Turn a weak or underperforming brand into a premium ad concept. Create the campaign idea, hook, script, visual direction, sound direction, and CTA — all tailored to the specific brand.

---

## Inputs

- Brand name
- Niche
- Current content (last 12 posts)
- Website or bio
- Competitor analysis
- Business goal (bookings, sales, awareness, launches)

---

## Process

1. **Diagnose the gap:** What's missing in their current creative?
2. **Identify the emotional opportunity:** What feeling does their audience want but isn't getting?
3. **Create the big idea:** One campaign concept that bridges the gap
4. **Design the hook:** 0-3 second grab that stops the scroll
5. **Write the 15-second script:** Full arc with beginning, middle, CTA
6. **Define visual direction:** Lighting, composition, motion, color
7. **Define sound direction:** Mood, tempo, key, instrumentation
8. **Set the CTA:** What exactly should the viewer do?
9. **Predict the outcome:** What business result should this ad create?

---

## Output

- Campaign name
- Big idea (1 sentence)
- 5-second hook script
- 15-second full script
- Visual direction brief
- Sound direction brief
- CTA
- Expected business outcome

---

## Quality Check

- [ ] Concept is specific to this brand, not generic
- [ ] Hook creates an emotional reaction in 3 seconds
- [ ] Script has a clear arc (setup → tension → resolution → CTA)
- [ ] Visual and sound directions are executable
- [ ] Expected outcome is quantifiable

---

## Example Use

**Brand:** Johannesburg restaurant with great food but weak content
**Gap:** They show food. They don't show the *feeling* of dining there.
**Emotional opportunity:** Exclusivity and social proof
**Big idea:** "The reservation you can't get"
**Hook:** "Everyone says they know the spot. This is the one they gatekeep."
**Script:** Packed dining room → text "8 tables. 200 DMs. 0 walk-ins." → "Book before Friday"
**Visual:** Warm candlelight, intimate framing, blurred happy diners
**Sound:** Ambient restaurant ambience + warm bass
**CTA:** Book before Friday
**Outcome:** Increase weekend bookings 30%+
