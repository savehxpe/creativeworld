# Comparison Desk Engine

Outworld Creative — Internal Skill  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Purpose

Create comparison-led content that teaches brands how attention, ads, sound, platforms, and culture actually work. Fact-driven, not conspiracy-driven.

---

## Inputs

- Two concepts to compare (e.g., small brand vs. big brand)
- Target audience (founders, marketers, creators)
- Platform (X, Instagram carousel, LinkedIn)
- Emotional goal (educate, provoke thought, shift perspective)

---

## Process

1. **Identify the real contrast:** What's the gap between what people think and what actually works?
2. **Frame the comparison:** Use clear "A vs. B" structure
3. **Add the insight:** Why does this gap exist? What's the business logic?
4. **Include the lesson:** What should the reader do with this information?
5. **Soft CTA:** How does this connect to Outworld's value?

**Fact rules:**
- Use public data (SEC filings, industry reports, platform statements)
- Use business logic, not theories
- Never claim secret control or unnamed groups
- Always source claims when possible

---

## Output

- X post (280-500 characters)
- Instagram carousel (7 slides)
- LinkedIn post (short form)
- Key insight (1 sentence summary)
- Soft CTA option

---

## Quality Check

- [ ] Comparison is based on facts or clear business logic
- [ ] No conspiracy language or unsourced claims
- [ ] Insight is actionable, not just interesting
- [ ] Tone is sharp but not aggressive
- [ ] Soft CTA connects naturally to the lesson

---
