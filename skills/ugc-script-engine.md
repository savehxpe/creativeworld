# UGC Script Engine

Outworld Creative — Internal Skill  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Purpose

Write ad scripts that feel like organic user-generated content — not boardroom-approved ads. The goal is trust, not polish.

---

## Inputs

- Brand name and product
- Niche
- Platform (Reels, TikTok, Stories)
- Target audience persona
- Brand voice (playful, serious, luxury, street)
- Any restrictions (claims, compliance, legal)

---

## Process

1. **Identify the real problem:** What does the customer actually struggle with?
2. **Find the relatable moment:** What everyday situation makes them need this product?
3. **Write the hook:** First 3 seconds must feel like a friend starting a story
4. **Build the story:** Problem → Discovery → Solution → Result
5. **Add the subtle CTA:** Not "BUY NOW" — more like "I linked it in my bio"
6. **Inject imperfection:** Deliberately include small flaws (lighting, pauses, "ums") that make it feel real
7. **Sound design:** What background noise, music, or ambient sound makes this feel authentic?

**UGC script rules:**
- No corporate language
- No script smell (over-rehearsed lines)
- No perfect lighting
- No boardroom-approved claims
- Real voice, real setting, real problem

---

## Output

- 3 hook options (0-3 seconds)
- Full 15-30 second script
- Visual direction notes (what to show, not how to light it)
- Sound direction notes (ambient, music, voice tone)
- CTA options (soft, medium, direct)
- Platform-specific adaptations

---

## Quality Check

- [ ] Script sounds like a real person, not a marketer
- [ ] Hook creates curiosity in 3 seconds
- [ ] Problem is relatable to the target audience
- [ ] CTA feels natural, not forced
- [ ] Script works for the specific platform (Reels vs. TikTok have different rhythms)

---

## Example Use

**Brand:** Skincare clinic
**Problem:** People are scared of facials because they don't know what to expect
**Relatable moment:** First-time nerves before a treatment
**Hook:** "I was so nervous for my first facial I almost cancelled."
**Script:**
- "I was so nervous for my first facial I almost cancelled."
- "But the therapist explained everything before touching my face."
- "No pain. Just 45 minutes of my skin actually breathing."
- "I booked my next one before I left. Link in bio if you're curious."
**Visual:** Handheld phone, natural bedroom light, no filters
**Sound:** Soft bedroom ambience, natural voice, no music
**CTA:** "Link in bio if you're curious"
