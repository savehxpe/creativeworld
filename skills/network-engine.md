# Network Engine

Outworld Creative — Internal Skill  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Purpose

Map who to reach, who connects to who, and which relationships can unlock rooms — clients, collaborators, referrals, and cultural gatekeepers.

---

## Inputs

- Target niche (restaurant, fashion, hospitality, etc.)
- Geographic market (Johannesburg, Cape Town, Dubai, global)
- Current network (who you already know)
- Goal (client acquisition, collaboration, press, investment)

---

## Process

1. **Identify hub nodes:** Who in this niche knows everyone else? (Influencers, event organizers, industry publication editors)
2. **Map bridges:** Who connects otherwise separate groups? (Cross-niche creators, multi-brand consultants)
3. **Find cold entry points:** Who is reachable via warm intro vs. who needs direct outreach?
4. **Prioritize by leverage:** Which connection gives the highest return for the least effort?
5. **Sequence the approach:** Who to contact first, second, third to build momentum?

---

## Output

- Network map (hub nodes, bridges, entry points)
- Prioritized contact list
- Recommended sequence of approach
- Draft introduction messages for warm intros

---

## Quality Check

- [ ] Map is based on real relationships, not imaginary connections
- [ ] Priorities consider both reach and accessibility
- [ ] Sequence builds momentum (each contact makes the next easier)
- [ ] Messages feel natural, not transactional

---

## Example Use

**Goal:** Land first 3 hospitality clients in Johannesburg
**Hub nodes:** Boutique hotel association, luxury travel bloggers, wedding venue coordinators
**Bridges:** A photographer who shoots for both restaurants and hotels
**Entry point:** Photographer warm intro → hotel owner → venue coordinator
**Sequence:**
1. Contact photographer (existing relationship)
2. Ask for intro to 2 hotel owners
3. Use hotel case study to approach venue coordinators
4. Use combined portfolio to approach hotel association
