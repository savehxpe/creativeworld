# Attention Engine

Outworld Creative — Internal Skill  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Purpose

Score how well an ad stops the scroll in the first 3 seconds. This is the most important moment in any short-form ad. If the hook fails, everything else is wasted.

---

## Inputs

- Ad concept or existing ad
- Target platform (Reels, TikTok, Stories, Feed)
- Target audience
- Niche (restaurant, fashion, hospitality, etc.)

---

## Process

1. **First 1-second test:** Does the ad create an emotional reaction (surprise, curiosity, desire, FOMO) within 1 second?
2. **First 3-second test:** Is the value proposition or hook clear by 3 seconds?
3. **Visual arrest test:** Is there something visually unexpected or pattern-breaking?
4. **Sound test:** Is there a sound element that enhances or drives the hook?
5. **Scroll resistance test:** Would someone watching this ad stop and watch past 3 seconds?

**Scoring:** 1-10 per test. Total 50. Below 30 = weak hook. 30-40 = decent. 40+ = strong.

---

## Output

- Attention score (0-50)
- Weakness analysis
- Specific improvements to the hook
- Alternative hook suggestions (3 options)

---

## Quality Check

- [ ] Score is based on actual viewer psychology, not subjective taste
- [ ] Alternative hooks are platform-native (not generic)
- [ ] Sound is considered as part of the hook, not an afterthought
- [ ] Recommendations are actionable in 1 sentence each

---

## Example Use

**Input:** Restaurant ad showing a plated dish with text overlay "Weekend Special"
**Score:** 22/50 (weak)
**Analysis:** Visual is generic food photography. No emotional trigger. No sound identity. No FOMO.
**Improvements:**
1. Open with hands reaching for the last plate + audio: "Only 3 left for Friday"
2. Show a packed dining room + text: "This table is never empty on weekends"
3. Close-up of a candlelit table + whisper: "The reservation you can't get"
