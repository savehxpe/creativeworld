# Revenue Leak Engine

Outworld Creative — Internal Skill  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Purpose

Find where a brand is losing money due to weak creative, missing conversion paths, or broken customer journeys. Quantify the loss in rands.

---

## Inputs

- Brand Instagram profile
- Website (if exists)
- Current booking/purchase flow
- Follower count and engagement rate
- Average transaction value
- Estimated monthly traffic (profile visits + website)

---

## Process

1. **Audit the funnel:** Profile visit → Content engagement → Action (book/buy/DM) → Conversion
2. **Identify friction points:** Where do people drop off?
3. **Calculate leakage:** How many potential customers are lost at each step?
4. **Quantify in rands:** Multiply lost customers by average transaction value
5. **Rank by impact:** Which leak is costing the most money?

**Common leaks:**
- No booking link in bio
- DM-to-order friction
- Weak hooks (people scroll past)
- No clear CTA
- Missing social proof
- Inconsistent posting (audience forgets you)

---

## Output

- Funnel audit with drop-off points
- Estimated monthly revenue loss (in rands)
- Top 3 highest-impact fixes
- Expected revenue recovery per fix

---

## Quality Check

- [ ] Numbers are conservative estimates, not exaggerated
- [ ] Math is shown step by step
- [ ] Fixes are ranked by impact, not ease
- [ ] Revenue recovery is realistic (20-40% improvement, not 10x)

---

## Example Use

**Brand:** Restaurant, 15K followers, no booking link
**Traffic:** 200 profile visits/day
**Leak:** 20% want to book but can't find how = 40 lost bookings/day
**Average:** R300/person, 2 people = R600/booking
**Daily loss:** 40 × R600 = R24,000/day = R168,000/week = R672,000/month
**Fix:** Add booking link + "Book" button in bio
**Expected recovery:** 50% of leak = R336,000/month
