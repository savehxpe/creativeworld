# Virality Engine

Outworld Creative — Internal Skill  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Purpose

Estimate whether a piece of content has the potential to spread organically using simple network math. Not a guarantee — a directional signal.

---

## Inputs

- Ad concept or content piece
- Platform (Reels, TikTok, Stories)
- Estimated share rate (how many viewers share it)
- Estimated comment rate (how many comment)
- Estimated save rate (how many save it)
- Emotional trigger (FOMO, humor, shock, desire, identity)

---

## Process

1. **K-factor estimate:** (Share rate × Average followers per sharer) / 100
2. **Network multiplier:** How many degrees of separation can it travel?
3. **Emotional intensity:** Does it create a strong enough feeling that someone *must* send it to someone else?
4. **Platform fit:** Is the format native to the platform's algorithm preferences?
5. **Timing:** Is the content relevant to current cultural moments?

**K-factor interpretation:**
- Below 0.5: Likely dies in the feed
- 0.5-1.0: Moderate organic reach
- Above 1.0: Viral potential (each share generates more than one new view)

---

## Output

- Estimated K-factor
- Virality likelihood (low / moderate / high)
- Why it would or wouldn't spread
- 3 adjustments to increase spread potential

---

## Quality Check

- [ ] Estimates are conservative, not optimistic
- [ ] Analysis considers platform algorithm behavior
- [ ] Emotional trigger is identified specifically
- [ ] Adjustments are concrete, not vague

---

## Example Use

**Input:** Fashion drop countdown video with bass drop and "47 pieces left" counter
**K-factor estimate:** 1.2 (high)
**Why:** Scarcity creates FOMO. Counter is shareable. Bass drop is memorable. Fashion audience has high follower counts.
**Adjustments to improve:**
1. Add a sound that becomes the "signature" of the drop
2. Create a challenge: "Post your fit before it sells out"
3. Release a "sold out" follow-up to capitalize on missed-demand energy
