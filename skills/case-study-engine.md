# Case Study Engine

Outworld Creative — Internal Skill  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Purpose

Track before/after, results, screenshots, testimonials, and learnings. Build a library of proof that makes future sales easier.

---

## Inputs

- Client name and niche
- Before state (content, metrics, creative quality)
- After state (new content, metrics, creative quality)
- Results (bookings, sales, engagement, follower growth)
- Testimonials (client quotes)
- Screenshots (before/after content)

---

## Process

1. **Document the before:** Screenshot current state. Record metrics.
2. **Document the after:** Screenshot new state. Record metrics.
3. **Calculate the delta:** What changed? By how much?
4. **Capture the testimonial:** Ask client for specific feedback.
5. **Extract the lesson:** What worked? What didn't? What would you do differently?
6. **Package the case study:** Before/after, results, quote, lesson.

**Case study format:**
- Client name (if permission granted) or "Anonymous [niche] brand"
- The problem
- The solution
- The results
- The testimonial
- The lesson

---

## Output

- Case study document (1 page)
- Before/after screenshots
- Results summary
- Testimonial quote
- Lesson learned
- Archive entry in memory engine

---

## Quality Check

- [ ] Before/after is visually clear
- [ ] Results are quantified (not "better," but "40% increase")
- [ ] Testimonial is specific, not generic
- [ ] Lesson is honest (includes what didn't work)
- [ ] Client has approved use of their name/content

---

## Example Use

**Client:** Restaurant in Johannesburg
**Before:** 12K followers, 2% engagement, no booking link, random food photos
**After:** 15K followers, 5% engagement, booking link added, "The Reservation You Can't Get" campaign
**Results:** Weekend bookings up 35% in 30 days
**Testimonial:** "We thought we just needed more posts. Outworld showed us we needed direction."
**Lesson:** The hook matters more than the frequency. One strong campaign beats 30 random posts.
