# Memory Engine

Outworld Creative — Internal Skill  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Purpose

Save what worked, what didn't, and why. Build a living library of creative direction knowledge that improves every week.

---

## Inputs

- Campaign results
- Client feedback
- Platform algorithm changes
- Creative tests (A/B results)
- Industry trends
- Personal observations

---

## Process

1. **Capture the signal:** What happened? What was the result?
2. **Tag it:** Niche, platform, format, emotion, outcome
3. **Analyze the pattern:** Does this work consistently? Under what conditions?
4. **Update the engines:** Feed insights back into attention engine, virality engine, spec ad engine, etc.
5. **Archive for reference:** Make it searchable for future projects

**What to save:**
- Winning hooks (what made them work)
- Failed concepts (why they failed)
- Client objections (and what overcame them)
- Platform changes (and how they affected performance)
- Sound directions (and which ones resonated)
- Visual directions (and which ones converted)

---

## Output

- Weekly learnings summary
- Updated engine parameters
- Tag library
- Searchable archive

---

## Quality Check

- [ ] Every entry has a clear result (win or loss)
- [ ] Tags are consistent and searchable
- [ ] Insights are fed back into active engines
- [ ] Archive is reviewed monthly
- [ ] No duplication — consolidate similar learnings

---

## Example Use

**Entry:** Restaurant "craving system" campaign
**Result:** 40% increase in profile-to-booking conversion
**Why it worked:** Hook created immediate hunger. Sound was appetizing. CTA was clear.
**Tags:** restaurant, hook, sound, conversion, FOMO
**Insight:** Food ads that make people hungry in 3 seconds convert 3x better than pretty food photos.
**Action:** Add "appetite trigger" as a mandatory attention engine test for all restaurant ads.
