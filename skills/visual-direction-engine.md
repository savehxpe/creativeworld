# Visual Direction Engine

Outworld Creative — Internal Skill  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Purpose

Define the visual rules that make every piece of content feel premium, consistent, and on-brand. Lighting, mood, colour, framing, motion, and platform-specific adaptations.

---

## Inputs

- Brand personality
- Target audience
- Niche
- Current content (what they post now)
- Competitor visuals (what others in the niche do)
- Platform (Reels, TikTok, Stories, Feed)

---

## Process

1. **Analyze current state:** What's working visually? What's hurting the brand?
2. **Define the visual identity:**
   - Lighting direction (natural, studio, neon, golden hour, moody)
   - Color palette (primary, secondary, accent, grading style)
   - Composition rules (subject placement, negative space, depth)
   - Texture and material references
   - Motion style (slow push-in, handheld, locked-off, dynamic)
3. **Create platform adaptations:** How the visual rules change for Reels vs. TikTok vs. Stories
4. **Build the moodboard:** 8-12 reference images that capture the direction
5. **Write the visual brief:** 1-page document that any creator can follow

---

## Output

- Visual direction brief (1 page)
- Color palette (hex codes + usage rules)
- Lighting guide (when to use what)
- Composition rules (safe zones, text placement, logo rules)
- Motion style guide
- Moodboard (8-12 references)

---

## Quality Check

- [ ] Direction is specific enough to execute, not vague
- [ ] Rules work across all platforms
- [ ] References are from real campaigns, not stock photos
- [ ] Direction elevates the brand above competitors
- [ ] Any creator can follow the brief and produce consistent work

---

## Example Use

**Brand:** Boutique hotel in Cape Town
**Personality:** Escape, luxury, quiet confidence
**Lighting:** Golden hour + soft diffused daylight. No harsh flashes.
**Color:** Warm amber, soft cream, natural greens, ocean blue accents
**Composition:** Wide shots showing space. Subject small in frame to emphasize escape.
**Motion:** Slow cinematic push-ins. No fast cuts. Let the viewer breathe.
**Moodboard:** References from Aman Resorts, Soho House, Kinfolk Magazine
