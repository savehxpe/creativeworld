# Proposal Engine

Outworld Creative — Internal Skill  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Purpose

Turn a Creative Diagnosis into a custom proposal that closes the client. No fixed packages. Every proposal is scoped to the brand's specific gaps and goals.

---

## Inputs

- Creative Diagnosis report
- Discovery call notes
- Brand goals and budget context
- Timeline and launch dates
- Competitor landscape

---

## Process

1. **Restate the gap:** "Based on our conversation, here's what we found..."
2. **Show the vision:** "Here's what your brand could look like with direction..."
3. **Define the scope:** Specific deliverables, timelines, and outcomes
4. **Present the investment:** Custom price based on scope (never a menu)
5. **Make it easy to say yes:** Clear next steps, no friction

**Proposal structure:**
- Executive summary (1 paragraph)
- Current state vs. desired state
- Scope of work (specific deliverables)
- Timeline
- Investment
- Next steps
- Terms (simple, no legal jargon)

---

## Output

- Custom proposal (PDF)
- Scope of work document
- Timeline
- Investment breakdown
- Next steps email

---

## Quality Check

- [ ] Proposal is specific to this brand, not a template with name changed
- [ ] Scope is realistic and achievable
- [ ] Timeline accounts for client feedback loops
- [ ] Investment is justified by the value, not by hours
- [ ] Next steps require minimal effort from client

---

## Example Use

**Brand:** Boutique hotel in Cape Town
**Gap:** Weak content, no sound identity, low weekend occupancy
**Vision:** "Wake Up Here" campaign with video ads and brand sound
**Scope:**
- Month 1: Brand sound identity + 3 spec ad concepts
- Month 2: Campaign launch (5 video ads + brand sound)
- Month 3: Performance review + optimization
**Investment:** Custom retainer based on value (not hourly)
**Next steps:** "Reply to confirm. We'll send the contract and first invoice."
