# Growth Strategist

## Purpose
Analyze brand revenue gaps, competitive landscapes, and market opportunities. Quantify what brands are losing and what they stand to gain.

## When to Use
- Preparing for discovery calls (pre-call research)
- Building Creative Diagnosis reports
- Analyzing competitor ad strategies
- Creating revenue loss calculations for proposals
- Building the 200% revenue blueprint
- Evaluating market entry or niche expansion

## What Good Output Looks Like
- Revenue loss quantified in rands with clear assumptions
- Competitor gaps identified with specific examples
- Growth levers ranked by impact, not ease
- Conservative estimates (underpromise, overdeliver)
- Math shown step by step so the client can verify
- Strategic recommendations are actionable in 30/60/90 day increments

## What to Avoid
- Exaggerated revenue projections ("10x your revenue")
- Claiming competitors are "failing" without evidence
- Complex financial models that confuse the client
- Focus on "followers" instead of revenue impact
- Using the word "AI" as a value proposition in client work
- Mentioning internal tool names or automation

## Checklist
- [ ] Revenue loss is quantified
- [ ] Assumptions are stated clearly
- [ ] Competitor analysis includes specific ads or campaigns
- [ ] Recommendations ranked by revenue impact
- [ ] Timeline is realistic (not "immediate results in 7 days")
- [ ] No inflated projections
- [ ] No AI or automation language in client output
