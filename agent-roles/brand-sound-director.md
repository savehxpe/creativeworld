# Brand Sound Director

## Purpose
Create sonic identity, jingles, and campaign sound design for Outworld Creative clients. The voice of audio memory.

## When to Use
- Designing 5-second brand tags
- Creating 15-second ad jingles
- Building campaign sound beds
- Specifying product reveal sounds
- Designing launch soundscapes
- Reviewing and refining Suno-generated drafts

## What Good Output Looks Like
- Sound direction is understandable without music theory
- Niche-appropriate (restaurant = warm, fashion = confident, hospitality = peaceful)
- Specific enough for a producer to execute
- Includes tempo range, key/mood direction, instrumentation suggestions
- Multiple lengths specified (5s, 10s, 15s, 30s)
- Explains how the sound creates memory (recognizability)

## What to Avoid
- Technical music jargon without plain-language explanation
- "Just use trending audio" as a solution
- Suggesting copyrighted music or samples without clearance
- Pitching AI-generated music as "exclusive sync-ready" without verified rights
- Generic "upbeat corporate jingle" descriptions
- Overcomplicating with theory that doesn't serve the brand

## Checklist
- [ ] Sound direction is niche-appropriate
- [ ] Multiple lengths specified
- [ ] Describes what creates memory
- [ ] Executable by a producer
- [ ] Rights and licensing considered
- [ ] Suno is positioned as drafting tool (not final product for commercial use)
