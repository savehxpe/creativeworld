# Localization Offer

Outworld Creative — Internal  
Branch: creative-director

---

## What It Is

Cultural translation pipelines that adapt existing ad campaigns for African markets. Take a global ad and make it feel native to Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa, Ghana, or pan-African audiences.

---

## The Problem

Global brands running ads in Africa often see lower conversion because their ads weren't designed for the market. The language feels off. The pacing is wrong. The music doesn't connect. The visuals feel foreign.

Translation is not localization. A translated ad is still a foreign ad.

---

## The Offer

```
Localization / Cultural Translation Pipeline

Adapt your existing ad campaigns for African markets through:

1. Language Adaptation
   - Translate with local idioms, not just words
   - Adjust tone for cultural context
   - Write local-language script variations

2. Rhythm & Music Adaptation
   - Replace soundtrack with locally resonant music
   - Adjust pacing to match local viewing behavior
   - Add culturally relevant sound cues

3. Visual Adaptation
   - Replace culturally mismatched visuals
   - Adjust color palettes for local preference
   - Cast talent that reflects the target market

4. Platform Adaptation
   - Optimize for platforms dominant in each market
   - Adjust aspect ratios and lengths
   - Adapt CTAs for local payment and booking behavior
```

## Why It Works

1. **Higher conversion:** Localized ads outperform translated ads by 30-50%
2. **Market entry value:** The cost of localization is a fraction of the revenue a new market opens
3. **Recurring need:** Every campaign needs localization. Not a one-time project.
4. **Moat:** Few agencies combine creative direction + cultural knowledge + sound + speed

## Delivery Format

- Adapted script per market
- Visual direction brief (what to change)
- Sound direction brief (replacement music/sounds)
- Platform-specific format guide
- Optional: Kie-generated stills using adapted visual direction
- Optional: Suno-generated sound pack per market
