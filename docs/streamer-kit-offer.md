# Streamer Kit Offer

Outworld Creative — Internal  
Branch: creative-director

---

## What It Is

Pre-built visual and sound packages for streamers who want a professional setup without hiring a designer.

---

## The Problem

Mid-sized streamers outgrow free templates but can't afford custom production. Their stream looks amateur, which hurts viewer retention and sponsorship opportunities.

---

## The Offer

```
Streamer Kit

All-in-one visual + sound package:

Visuals:
- 3 overlay templates (starting soon, BRB, stream ending)
- 2 transition animations (scene switch, cam switch)
- 1 stream intro/outro animation
- Alert box template
- Chat box template

Sounds:
- 3 alert sounds (follow, sub, donation)
- 2 transition sounds
- 1 stream intro loop (15 seconds)
- 1 brand sound tag (3 seconds)

Optional: Custom kit with streamer's branding.
```

---

## Pricing (Internal)

- Standard Kit (pre-made): R300–R800
- Premium Kit (semi-custom): R1,000–R2,500
- Custom Kit (fully branded): R2,500–R5,000

---

## Target Markets

| Streamer Type | Pain | Kit Focus |
|---------------|------|-----------|
| Gaming streamers | "My alerts sound basic" | Alert sounds + overlays |
| Music streamers | "I need pro audio transitions" | Sound-focused kits |
| Podcast streamers | "My video stream looks amateur" | Overlay + intro kits |
| IRL streamers | "I need quick scene transitions" | Transition-focused kits |

---

## Delivery Format

- PNG overlays (transparent, ready for OBS/Streamlabs)
- WAV + MP3 sounds
- Simple setup guide (how to add to streaming software)
- Usage license

---

## Why It's #4 (Not #1)

- Smaller market than ad creatives and localization
- Lower price point
- One-time purchase, not recurring
- Good for portfolio and word-of-mouth
- Not the main revenue driver

Build this opportunistically. Don't prioritize over Ad Creative Packs or Localization.

---

## Quality Gates

- [ ] Overlays work at standard streaming resolutions (1080p)
- [ ] Sounds are short and loop-ready
- [ ] Setup guide is simple enough for a non-technical streamer
- [ ] Every sound passes `audio-rights-check`
- [ ] Usage license is included
