# CapCut Template Factory

Outworld Creative — Internal  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Overview

The Template Factory creates reusable short-form ad template blueprints. These are creative specs — not actual CapCut files — that define timeline structure, beat markers, text and visual placeholders, CTA positioning, and sound direction.

---

---

## How Templates Are Used

Templates are given to clients or creators with instructions to:
1. Replace placeholder visuals with brand assets
2. Replace placeholder text with brand copy
3. Replace placeholder sound with brand sound pack
4. Export at 9:16, 1080p
5. Test on platform before scaling
