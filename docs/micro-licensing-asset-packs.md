# Micro-Licensing Asset Packs

Outworld Creative — Internal  
Branch: creative-director

---

## What It Is

Pre-made, downloadable packs of sounds, loops, textures, and visual assets sold with simple usage licenses. Passive revenue — build once, sell many times.

---

## The Problem

Content creators, indie game developers, podcasters, and small video editors need professional assets but can't afford custom production. They buy from stock marketplaces or use free tiers that everyone else uses.

Outworld packs can be more niche, more culturally relevant, and more stylistically consistent than generic stock.

## Critical Rules

- [ ] **Only sell assets with confirmed commercial rights.**
- [ ] Every sound must pass the `audio-rights-check` skill.
- [ ] Record generation details: prompt, date, model, account tier, task ID.
- [ ] Usage license must be clear: what the buyer can and cannot do.
- [ ] Free-tier Suno outputs are NOT eligible for commercial sale.
- [ ] AI-generated works should not be claimed as "copyrighted."
- [ ] License should grant usage rights, not "ownership" or "exclusive rights."
- [ ] Buyer receives a simple, readable license — not a 20-page legal document.
