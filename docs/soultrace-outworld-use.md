# Soultrace — Outworld Creative Use

Internal document — not for public distribution

---

## Purpose

Soultrace provides a 5-color psychological model (White=structure, Blue=understanding, Black=agency, Red=intensity, Green=connection) with Bayesian adaptive question selection to classify users into one of 25 archetypes.

At Outworld, we use Soultrace as a brand psychology and audience archetype layer. It helps us:

- Define the Outworld Creative brand personality
- Define saveHXPE's founder voice
- Define client archetypes
- Define audience emotional triggers
- Shape campaign tone
- Prevent the site from sounding generic
- Make brand strategy sharper

---

## Usage Rules

### Allowed
- Brand personality profiling
- Founder voice archetyping
- Client persona development
- Audience emotional trigger mapping
- Campaign tone calibration

### Not Allowed
- Public fake psychological claims
- Diagnosing people
- Manipulating users
- Medical or mental health claims
- Replacing real market research

## Archetype Workflow

### 1. Outworld Creative Archetype
Define the agency's own personality archetype. What does Outworld sound like? What does Outworld feel like?
Output: `docs/outworld-brand-archetype.md`

### 2. saveHXPE Founder Voice Archetype
Define the founder's creative personality. What drives saveHXPE's taste? What makes his creative direction distinctive?
Output: incorporated into `docs/outworld-brand-archetype.md`

### 3. Ideal Client Archetypes
Define 4 client personas:
- Fashion/clothing brand owner
- Restaurant/hospitality owner
- Music/creator brand
- Fintech/app brand

### 4. Content Tone
Outworld's voice calibration:
- Calm — not frantic, not desperate
- Sharp — not vague, not filler
- Premium — not cheap, not discount
- Culturally aware — not generic
- Commercially useful — not abstract
