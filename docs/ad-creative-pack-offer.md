# Ad Creative Pack Offer

Outworld Creative — Internal  
Branch: creative-director

---

## What It Is

High-volume short-form ad creative packs for brands, apps, ecommerce, and agencies that need to test creative angles fast.

---

## The Problem

Brands need to test multiple hooks, formats, and angles to find what converts. Most can only produce 2-3 ad variations per week. By the time they find a winning hook, the trend has passed.

---

## The Offer

```
Ad Creative Pack

10-50 short-form ad variations per campaign.

Each variation includes:
- 5-second hook concept
- 15-second ad script
- Visual direction (lighting, composition, motion)
- Sound direction (tag, loop, or reveal sound)
- Caption
- CTA
- Platform-specific adaptation notes (Reels / TikTok / Stories)
```

---

## Pricing (Internal)

- 10 variations: R2,000–R5,000 (test pack)
- 25 variations: R5,000–R10,000 (standard pack)
- 50 variations: R10,000–R20,000 (full campaign pack)
- Monthly retainer (ongoing testing): R8,000–R15,000/month

## Delivery Format

- Google Doc or Notion page per campaign
- Visual mockups (Kie-generated stills) for top 5 variations
- Sound direction briefs for top 5 variations
- Optional: Kie-generated stills for all variations
- Optional: Seedance animated previews for top 3

---

## Why It Works

Brands buy this because:
1. **Speed:** 50 variations in days, not weeks
2. **Volume:** More tests = faster winner discovery
3. **Consistency:** Every variation follows brand sound + visual direction
4. **Platform-native:** Each variation is adapted for the specific platform
