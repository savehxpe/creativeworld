Outworld Creative  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Philosophy

Suno is a drafting engine, not a final product factory. Use it to explore mood, tempo, and direction — then recreate and refine with your own production skills.

**Never pitch AI-generated music as fully exclusive sync-ready unless you have verified commercial rights.**

---

## The Safe Workflow

```
Step 1: Draft mood and direction in Suno (reference only, non-commercial use)
Step 2: Export the reference track
Step 3: Recreate with your own production (drums, melodies, vocals, tags, mix)
Step 4: Add your own sonic identity (producer tags, unique textures, brand voice)
Step 5: Export multiple lengths: 5s, 10s, 15s, 30s
Step 6: Deliver to client with simple usage license
```

**Critical:** Suno's free/basic tier outputs are for personal, non-commercial use. For paid client work, you must either:
- Use their Pro/Business tier with verified commercial rights
- Or (preferred) use Suno only as inspiration, then produce the final audio yourself

## Per-Niche Sound Direction

### Restaurants
- **Mood:** Warm, appetizing, social
- **Sounds:** Food reveal stings, sizzle textures, booking urgency beds, ambient dining atmosphere
- **Tempo:** 90-110 BPM for appetite appeal
- **Key:** Major keys, warm tones

### Fashion / Streetwear
- **Mood:** Confident, trendy, status-driven
- **Sounds:** Drop countdown beats, walk rhythms, collection soundtracks, bass stings
- **Tempo:** 120-140 BPM for energy
- **Key:** Minor keys for edge, major for aspiration

### Hospitality
- **Mood:** Escape, peace, premium simplicity
- **Sounds:** Nature ambience + soft pads, weekend reset beds, sunrise textures
- **Tempo:** 70-90 BPM for relaxation
- **Key:** Major keys, airy, open

### Beauty / Aesthetics
- **Mood:** Trust, transformation, soft luxury
- **Sounds:** Soft reveal sounds, gentle build-ups, testimonial beds
- **Tempo:** 80-100 BPM for calm confidence
- **Key:** Major keys, soft pads, minimal percussion

### Events / Nightlife
- **Mood:** Energy, FOMO, urgency
- **Sounds:** Hype stingers, bass drops, lineup teasers, recap beats
- **Tempo:** 128-150 BPM for peak energy
- **Key:** Minor keys, heavy bass, rhythmic hooks

### Fintech / Digital Products
- **Mood:** Trust, clarity, modern
- **Sounds:** Clean interface tones, notification cues, app audio identity
- **Tempo:** 100-120 BPM for focus
- **Key:** Neutral, clean, minimal

### Real Estate
- **Mood:** Aspiration, lifestyle, location
- **Sounds:** Location soundscapes, property reveal beds, lifestyle ambience
- **Tempo:** 80-100 BPM for contemplation
- **Key:** Major keys, spacious, cinematic

## Export Checklist

- [ ] 5-second version (brand tag / sting)
- [ ] 10-second version (short hook)
- [ ] 15-second version (ad bed)
- [ ] 30-second version (extended bed / loop)
- [ ] WAV format (uncompressed for client)
- [ ] MP3 format (compressed for social)
- [ ] Usage license document included
- [ ] Producer credit: "Brand sound by Outworld Creative"
