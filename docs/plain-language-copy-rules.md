# Plain Language Copy Rules

Outworld Creative — Internal  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Purpose

Keep Outworld Creative copy clear, premium, direct, and easy to understand in under 5 seconds. No jargon. No buzzwords. No pitch-deck language.

---

## The 5-Second Rule

If a business owner can't understand what Outworld sells in 5 seconds of reading, the copy fails.

---

## Words to Avoid

| Word/Phrase | Why | Replacement |
|-------------|-----|-------------|
| creative intelligence | Sounds like a consultancy, not a creative studio | direction, ideas, notes |
| ecosystem | Tech jargon. Nobody buys an ecosystem. | system, setup |
| infrastructure | B2B SaaS language. Cold. | tools, setup |
| scalable | Everyone says this. Meaningless. | can grow with you |
| automated | Triggers "cheap AI" perception | built, made, created |
| engine | Sounds mechanical. Outworld is creative. | skill, tool |
| sonic ads | Industry language. Clients don't search this. | brand sound |
| AI-powered | Useful once for credibility. Not 5 times. | smart, fast, built |
| conversion architecture | Nobody talks like this. | clear path to buy |
| proprietary | Legal-sounding. Pretentious. | our own |
| revolutionary | Hype word. Weakens trust. | new, better, different |
| algorithmic | Sounds like a scam. | smart, automatic |
| daily intelligence | Sounds like a spy agency. | simple notes, quick reads |
| high-arousal | Too technical for a brand owner | scroll-stopping, attention-grabbing |

---

## Words to Prefer

These words are searchable, understandable, and premium:

video ads, brand sound, jingles, hooks, campaigns, launch visuals, content ideas, Reels, TikTok, Shorts, remembered, seen, heard, sharper, clear, simple, premium, trusted

---

## Tone Guidelines

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Short sentences (max 20 words) | Long paragraphs |
| One idea per sentence | Multiple ideas crammed together |
| Direct ("We make short ads.") | Passive ("Short-form ad assets are created.") |
| Confident but calm | Hype or desperation |
| Premium but simple | Academic or corporate |
| Specific ("5-second hooks") | Vague ("next-level creative solutions") |

---

## Section Rules

- **Hero:** Under 30 words. What we do. Who it's for.
- **What We Create:** Clean card titles + single-sentence descriptions
- **Spec Ad Lab:** Proof, not promises
- **Brand Sound:** Simple. The message is "most brands look good, few sound good."
- **Signal Desk:** Notes, not reports. Useful, not academic.
- **CTA:** One clear action. No friction.
