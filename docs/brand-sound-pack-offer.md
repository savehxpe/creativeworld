# Brand Sound Pack Offer

Outworld Creative — Internal  
Branch: creative-director

---

## What It Is

A 3-part brand sound pack for brands using random trending sounds but no memorable audio identity.

---

## The Problem

Most brands use whatever sound is trending on Reels or TikTok. That means:
- Every ad starts from zero
- No sonic memory is built
- The platform benefits more than the brand

The brands that win have a consistent sound identity. Netflix, McDonald's, iPhone, PlayStation — recognized before the logo appears.

---

## The Offer

```
Brand Sound Pack

3 short sounds for your brand:

1. 3-Second Brand Tag
   Your audio identity. Recognizable in 3 seconds.

2. 15-Second Ad Loop
   For Reels, TikTok, and social ads. Loops seamlessly.

3. 10-Second Intro/Outro
   For podcasts, long-form content, and brand films.

Optional: Product reveal sound, launch sound, campaign music idea.
```

## Delivery Format

- WAV (uncompressed) + MP3 (compressed) for each sound
- Usage license (territory, duration, media)
- Rights confirmation via `audio-rights-check` skill
- Producer credit: "Brand sound by Outworld Creative"

---

## Critical Rules

- [ ] Only sell sounds generated under a commercial-use eligible Suno account (Pro/Premier)
- [ ] Every sound must pass the `audio-rights-check` skill before delivery
- [ ] Keep records: prompt, date, model, account tier, task ID
- [ ] Client receives a usage license, not "ownership"
- [ ] Producer must edit/finish the output before delivery
- [ ] Never claim copyright registration for AI-generated audio
- [ ] Never call the output "exclusive" unless the license allows it and the file has been meaningfully customized
