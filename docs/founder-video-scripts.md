# Founder Video Scripts

Outworld Creative — Teleprompter Scripts  
Branch: creative-director

## Purpose

5 short founder video scripts. Under 60 seconds each. Calm, technical, useful. Show code/output beside the speaker. No hype, no fake energy, no overproduction.

---

## Script 1: Ad Fatigue

**Duration:** 45 seconds  
**Setup:** Split screen — speaker on left, scrolling ad variations on right.

```
Most brands run the same ad for weeks.

Then they wonder why performance drops.

That's ad fatigue.

Your audience has seen your ad 15 times.
They've memorized the hook.
They scroll past it.

The fix isn't a bigger budget.
The fix is creative variation.

[Show: 10 ad variations with different hooks, visuals, sound]

One product. Ten directions.
Built to fight ad fatigue.

Built by Outworld Creative.
For brand work: team@outworldcreative.com
```

## Production Notes

- All scripts under 60 seconds
- Tone: calm, confident, founder-led — not influencer energy
- Show real output beside speaker (not stock footage)
- Single camera, natural lighting, clean audio
- CTA consistent: "Built by Outworld Creative. For brand work: team@outworldcreative.com"
- No music bed — let silence do the work between phrases
