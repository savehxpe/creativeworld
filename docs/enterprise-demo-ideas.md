## Demo 1: Ad-Fatigue Fighter

**Problem:** Brands and agencies need 10-50 ad variations for creative testing. Most can only produce 2-3 per week. By the time they find a winning hook, the trend has passed.

**Input:** One product or brand  
**Output:**
- 10 ad variations
- 10 hooks
- 10 visual directions
- 10 sound directions

**Public line:** "One product. Ten creative angles. Built to fight ad fatigue."

**Demo format:** Single-page demo site or Notion doc showing all 10 variations side by side. Each with hook, visual preview (Kie still), sound direction, and CTA variation.

**Assets needed:** 10 Kie stills, 10 Suno sound directions, 1 demo page.

---

## Demo 2: Localization Pipeline

**Problem:** Global brands running ads in Africa often see lower conversion because their ads weren't designed for the market. Language, pacing, music, and visuals feel foreign.

**Input:** One generic ad concept  
**Output:** 5 localized ad directions for:
- South Africa
- Nigeria
- Kenya
- UAE
- Europe/global

**Public line:** "Same product. Different market. Different rhythm."

**Demo format:** Comparison grid showing the original generic ad vs. the 5 localized versions. Each showing adapted script, visual direction, music direction, and CTA.

**Assets needed:** 5 localization adaptor outputs, optional 5 Kie stills per market.

---

## Demo 3: App Sound Identity

**Problem:** Most apps have visuals. Almost none have a consistent sound identity. Every notification, error, and success moment is generic platform audio — or silent.

**Input:** Fake fintech or mobile app interface (e.g., "Mali — Send Money Instantly")  
**Output:**
- Payment success sound
- Notification sound
- Error sound
- Onboarding loop

**Public line:** "Most apps have a look. Few have a sound."

**Demo format:** Interactive demo page or video showing the app interface with custom sounds playing at each interaction point.

**Assets needed:** 4 Suno-generated sounds, 1 demo video or HTML page.
