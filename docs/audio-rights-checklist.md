# Audio Rights Checklist

Outworld Creative — Legal Gate  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Purpose

Check whether generated audio is safe to sell before delivering to any client. This is a mandatory gate — no audio asset leaves Outworld without passing this checklist.

---

## The Checklist

### 1. Account Eligibility
- [ ] Was this generated under a commercial-use eligible Suno account? (Pro or Premier)
- [ ] Was the account in Pro/Premier status at the time of generation?

**Free-tier songs are for non-commercial use only.** Subscribing later does NOT grant retroactive commercial rights to songs made on the free plan.

### 2. Generation Records
- [ ] Is there a record of the prompt text used?
- [ ] Is there a record of the generation date?
- [ ] Is there a record of the model version (V5)?
- [ ] Is there a record of the task ID?
- [ ] Is the account tier documented?

### 3. Content Safety
- [ ] Is the output free from copied lyrics or known melodies?
- [ ] Were NO artist names used in the prompt?
- [ ] Were NO existing jingles or brand sounds imitated?
- [ ] Is the output original enough to not trigger infringement concerns?

### 4. Client Delivery
- [ ] Is the client receiving a usage license (not "ownership")?
- [ ] Are the license terms clear (territory, duration, media)?
- [ ] Has the output been edited or finished by the producer before delivery?
- [ ] Does the finished product contain meaningful human authorship (editing, mixing, arrangement)?

### 5. Legal Disclaimers
- [ ] Do NOT promise copyright registration for AI-generated audio.
- [ ] Do NOT call the output "exclusive" unless the license allows it AND the file has been meaningfully customized.
- [ ] Do NOT claim the sound is "royalty-free" unless you control all rights.
- [ ] Do NOT sell sounds that contain recognizable samples without clearance.

---

## Pass/Fail

- **ALL items must pass.** If any item fails, the sound is NOT cleared for commercial delivery.
- Failed sounds: use only as internal reference or portfolio proof (non-commercial).
- Re-generate with a commercial-eligible account if needed.
