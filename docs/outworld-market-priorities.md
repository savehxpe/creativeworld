# Outworld Market Priorities

Internal Strategy Document  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Core Positioning

Outworld Creative focuses on high-volume short-form ad creatives and brand sound for brands, apps, ecommerce companies, creators, and agencies.

---

## Language Rule

**Never say:** "manipulate algorithms," "game the system," "hack the feed," "secret algorithm trick"

**Always say:**
- test creative angles
- find winning hooks
- improve scroll-stop rate
- increase bookings, orders, installs, DMs, or leads
- optimize for platform performance
- iterate on creative direction

---

## Market Priority Ranking

### 1. High-Converting TikTok/Reels Ad Creatives

**Priority:** Primary lane. Fastest path to revenue.

**Target:** Ecommerce stores, DTC brands, mobile apps, fashion drops, beauty brands, restaurants, hotels, growth marketing agencies.

**Pain they pay for:** "We need more ad variations to test, but we can't produce them fast enough."

**Offer:** 10–50 short-form ad variations per campaign. Each includes hooks, visuals, motion prompts, captions, and sound directions.

**Revenue model:** Per-campaign pricing based on volume. Monthly retainer for ongoing creative testing.

**Why first:** High volume, repeatable, clear ROI for the client, scales with Kie + Suno pipeline.

---

### 2. Localization / Cultural Translation Pipelines

**Priority:** Premium later. Higher ticket, longer sales cycle.

**Target:** Fintech apps, mobile games, streaming platforms, telecoms, tourism boards, fashion brands, and global brands entering African markets.

**Pain they pay for:** "Our ads work in Europe/US but don't convert in Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa."

**Offer:** Adapt existing ad campaigns through language, rhythm, music, pacing, visuals, and cultural context. Take a global ad and make it feel native to a specific African market.

**Revenue model:** Project-based. Premium pricing justified by market access value.

**Why second:** Higher per-client value but longer pipeline. Build case studies with Market 1 first.

---

### 3. Micro-Licensing Asset Packs

**Priority:** Build in background. Passive revenue stream.

**Target:** Content creators, video editors, game developers, podcasters, indie brands.

**Pain they pay for:** "I need professional sounds and visuals but can't afford custom production."

**Offer:** Loop packs, stingers, background music, overlays, visual textures, product reveal sounds. Sold as downloadable packs with simple usage license.

**Revenue model:** One-time purchase per pack. Low price (R50-R200 per pack), high volume.

**Critical rule:** Only sell assets with confirmed commercial rights. Every asset must pass the `audio-rights-check` skill.

**Why third:** Scales without active client work. Build while Markets 1 and 2 generate cash.

---

### 4. Streamer Kits

**Priority:** Side offer. Opportunistic.

**Target:** Mid-sized streamers, gaming creators, music streamers, podcast streamers.

**Pain they pay for:** "My stream looks amateur. I need pro-level overlays and sounds without hiring a designer."

**Offer:** Overlays, alert sounds, transition animations, stream intro loops, brand sound kit.

**Revenue model:** One-time purchase per kit. Optional custom kit for premium.

**Why fourth:** Smaller market, lower price point. Good for portfolio and word-of-mouth. Not the main revenue driver.

---

## Revenue Ladder

```
Ad Creative Packs (fastest, repeatable)
    ↓
Localization Pipelines (higher ticket, longer cycle)
    ↓
Micro-Licensing Packs (passive, scales)
    ↓
Streamer Kits (side revenue, portfolio)
```

---

## What NOT to Sell

- "AI videos" or "AI content" — sells the wrong thing
- "Algorithm hacks" — sounds untrustworthy
- "Cheap ads" — attracts low-budget clients
- "Fast followers" — not a real business outcome
