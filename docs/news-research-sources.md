## How to Use Sources

1. **Skim headlines** (2 min) — look for anything surprising or counterintuitive
2. **Read 1-2 articles deeply** (5 min) — extract the insight that applies to brands
3. **Translate to brand lesson** (3 min) — turn industry news into actionable advice
4. **Draft post** (5 min) — apply the X or carousel format

**Rule:** Never just repost news. Always add the Outworld Creative angle: what this means for brands, and what they should do about it.
