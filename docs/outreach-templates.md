## Philosophy

The goal of every outreach message is **not to sell a retainer immediately**.  
The goal is to **get permission to send the spec ad preview**.

Every message must:
1. Show you've actually looked at their brand
2. Offer value first (the spec ad preview)
3. Position you as a creative director, not a service provider
4. Create curiosity, not pressure
5. Make it easy to say yes

**Never mention:** prices, packages, AI, automation, bots, "like [agency name]"

## Target Research Methodology

### How to Find 10 Restaurants
1. Instagram location search: "Johannesburg restaurant" / "Cape Town restaurant"
2. Filter: Has website or booking link, posts 3+ times/week, 5K-50K followers
3. Qualify: Does their content look like phone snapshots? Are they not running obvious campaigns?
4. Save handles to batch file

### How to Find 10 Fashion Brands
1. Instagram hashtags: #southafricanfashion #sastreetwear #saboutique
2. Filter: Online store or DM-to-order, 3K-30K followers, recent drops
3. Qualify: Is their launch content weak? Do they rely only on influencer reposts?
4. Save handles

### How to Find 10 Hospitality Properties
1. Google Maps: "boutique hotel [city]" / "guesthouse [city]" / "lodge [region]"
2. Check Instagram presence and website quality
3. Filter: 1K-20K followers, visible booking capability, under-optimized content
4. Save handles
