# saveHXPE Founder Voice

Outworld Creative + saveHXPE — Internal  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Purpose

Define the tone and boundaries for saveHXPE as the founder voice behind Outworld Creative content. Every public piece from saveHXPE must feel calm, credible, and source-backed — never desperate, over-jargoned, or fake controversial.

---

## Tone

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Simple. Short sentences. | Complex, academic, or jargon-heavy |
| Sharp. One clear point per post. | Vague platitudes about "the future" |
| Calm. Confident delivery, no rush. | Hype, urgency, or fake energy |
| Credible. Source-backed or experience-backed. | Made-up statistics or fake case studies |
| Source-backed. "According to [source]..." | "I heard somewhere..." |
| Personal POV. "From producing, I learned..." | Pretending to be an economist or analyst |
| Useful. Actionable. | Abstract theory or philosophy |
| Proof-led. Show what was built. | Promise what will be built |

---

## What saveHXPE Talks About

- Music production applied to brand building
- Why sound matters in advertising
- Creative direction vs. content creation
- Building Outworld Creative — lessons learned
- Culture, attention, and what brands miss
- Industry trends backed by public sources

## What saveHXPE Does NOT Talk About

- Personal finances, debt, or income
- Other people's business or drama
- Conspiracy theories or "secret control"
- Hot takes on politics or religion
- Clickbait or fake controversy
- Unsourced claims about "the industry"
- Complaints about not being recognized

---

## Example Voice

**Good:**
> "Most brands post products. The best brands build memory. That's the difference between a like today and a customer tomorrow. I learned this producing records — the hook is not the drop. The build-up is what makes the drop matter. Same with ads."

**Bad:**
> "The entire advertising industry is sleeping on sound. Nobody gets it. I'm about to change everything. Watch this space."
