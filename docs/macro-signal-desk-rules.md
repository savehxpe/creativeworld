# Macro Signal Desk Rules

Outworld Creative — Internal  
Branch: creative-director

---

## Purpose

Define the boundaries for public macro content. Outworld Signal Desk can cover industry shifts, platform changes, and economic trends — but must stay factual, useful, and free of conspiracy, fear, or fake predictions.

---

## What Is Allowed

- Reference public data: SEC filings, industry reports, platform official statements
- Explain business logic: how platforms make money, why algorithms prioritize certain content
- Connect macro shifts to creative opportunities: "Platform X is prioritizing short-form video — brands need stronger hooks"
- Use real market data: Statista, DataReportal, IAB, PwC, Mastercard

---

## What Is NOT Allowed

- **Conspiracy language:** No "they run the world," "secret control," "hidden hands," occult claims, or unnamed power groups
- **Financial predictions:** No "this stock will crash," "the economy is about to collapse," "get out before they take everything"
- **Fake expertise:** No claiming to predict market movements or currency shifts
- **Fear content:** No "you're being manipulated," "they're hiding this from you," "wake up"
- **Unverifiable claims:** No statistics without sources, no "insider information," no anonymous whistleblower narratives

---

## How to Frame Macro Content

**Instead of:** "They're controlling what you see."  
**Say:** "Platforms optimize for engagement. Understanding that helps brands build better creative."

**Instead of:** "The economy is rigged."  
**Say:** "Current market conditions favor brands with strong creative systems. Here's why."

**Instead of:** "Secret groups control advertising."  
**Say:** "Public platforms are shaped by founders, shareholders, advertisers, and user behavior."

---

## Turn Macro Shifts Into Creative Opportunities

| Macro Signal | Creative Opportunity |
|-------------|---------------------|
| Platform X prioritizing short-form video | Brands need 5-15 second ad systems |
| Creator ad spend growing 4x faster than industry | Brands need UGC-style ad production |
| Mobile-first internet in Africa | Brands need vertical, mobile-optimized creative |
| AI reducing production costs | Brands can test more creative angles faster |

---

## Verification

Before publishing any macro Signal Desk content, ask:

- [ ] Is this sourced from a public, verifiable report or statement?
- [ ] Does this help a brand understand a real opportunity?
- [ ] Is there zero conspiracy or fear language?
- [ ] Would a serious business person find this useful, not alarming?
- [ ] Can I link to the source if asked?

If any answer is no → don't publish.
