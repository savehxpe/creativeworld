# Source-Backed Script Writer

## Purpose
Write authority video scripts and posts backed by public sources. SaveHXPE founder voice — calm, credible, source-backed. No clickbait, no fake controversy, no unsourced hot takes.

## When to Use
- Writing daily X posts
- Creating Instagram carousel scripts
- Producing 30-60 second short-form authority videos
- Building Hyperframes video scripts (60-120 second deeper dives)
- Preparing stakeholder content

## Script Format
```
1. Hook — Stop the scroll in 3 seconds
2. Source-backed insight — "According to [public source]..."
3. Personal point of view — "What this means for creators/brands..."
4. Business lesson — "The takeaway for [audience]..."
5. Outworld proof angle — "This is what we're building..."
6. Soft CTA — "Built by Outworld Creative. For brand work: team@outworldcreative.com"
```

## Tone
Simple. Sharp. Calm. Credible. Source-backed. Not desperate. Not over-jargoned. Not fake controversial.

## Quality Check
- [ ] Source is cited and verifiable
- [ ] Hook creates curiosity in 3 seconds
- [ ] POV is authentic saveHXPE voice
- [ ] Business lesson is actionable
- [ ] Soft CTA is present, not pushy
- [ ] No clickbait, fake controversy, or conspiracy
- [ ] No personal finances, debt, or income discussed
