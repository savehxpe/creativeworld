# Spec Ad Lab

## Purpose
Generate Outworld Spec Ad Lab concepts for restaurants, fashion, hospitality, beauty, events, and digital products. Each concept is a complete campaign brief that shows proof of creative direction.

## When to Use
- Building a new spec ad for the Spec Ad Lab portfolio
- Creating client-specific spec ad concepts during a Creative Diagnosis
- Generating campaign ideas before requesting visual assets
- Expanding the Spec Ad Lab into new niches

## Output Structure

Each spec ad concept must include:
1. **Campaign Name** — Memorable, specific, evocative
2. **Niche** — Restaurant, fashion, hospitality, beauty, events, fintech
3. **Goal** — Specific business outcome (bookings, sales, awareness)
4. **Ad Style** — Conversion/FOMO, Cinematic Brand, Social Proof, Product Desire, etc.
5. **Emotional Trigger** — FOMO, desire, escape, urgency, social proof, status
6. **5-Second Hook** — The scroll-stopping opener
7. **15-Second Script** — Full arc with beginning, middle, CTA
8. **Visual Concept** — What the viewer sees
9. **Sonic Direction** — What the viewer hears (5-sec tag, sound bed, reveal sound)
10. **CTA** — What the viewer should do
11. **Business Outcome** — What this ad creates for the brand
12. **Image Prompt** — Ready for Nano Banana 2
13. **Video Prompt** — Ready for Seedance 1.5 Pro (if animated)

## Rules
- Concepts must be specific to the niche — not generic
- Hooks must create an emotional reaction in 3 seconds
- Visual and sound directions must be executable
- Business outcomes must be quantifiable (not "more engagement")
- No pricing, no AI language in the spec card itself
- Every spec card includes "Spec creative direction by Outworld Creative"

## Quality Check
- [ ] Campaign name is evocative and specific
- [ ] Hook works in 3 seconds or less
- [ ] Script has clear arc (hook → story → CTA)
- [ ] Visual direction matches campaign goal
- [ ] Sound direction is included and specific
- [ ] Business outcome is stated
