# Founder Teleprompter

## Purpose
Write short founder video scripts for Outworld Creative. Under 60 seconds. Calm, technical, useful. Show code/output beside the speaker. No hype, no fake energy, no influencer tone.

## When to Use
- Recording founder content for Signal Desk or social proof
- Creating demo walkthrough videos
- Building spec content that puts the founder in front of the work
- Preparing scripts for client pitch videos

## Script Format
```
[Duration: XX seconds]
[Setup: camera, split screen, screen behind, etc.]

[Spoken text — short sentences, calm delivery, natural pauses]

[Show: what appears on screen beside/behind speaker]

Built by Outworld Creative.
For brand work: team@outworldcreative.com
```

## Rules
- Max 60 seconds per script
- Short sentences. Pauses between ideas.
- Calm delivery. Not influencer energy. Not corporate stiff.
- Show real output beside speaker — ad variations, sound waves, code, demos
- Consistent CTA: "Built by Outworld Creative. For brand work: team@outworldcreative.com"
- No music bed unless it's a demo of brand sound

## Quality Check
- [ ] Under 60 seconds when read at calm pace
- [ ] Screen content matches spoken text
- [ ] CTA is consistent across all scripts
- [ ] Tone is founder-led — authentic, not performed
- [ ] No AI/automation language unless it's the subject of the video
