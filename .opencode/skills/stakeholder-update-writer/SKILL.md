# Stakeholder Update Writer

## Purpose
Write clear, calm, proof-led weekly updates for Dre Moon, Andy, Radical Publishing, and future stakeholders. Show commercial progress without begging. Let the work speak.

## When to Use
- Weekly (same day, same time) — consistency builds trust
- After significant milestones (new client, new asset, new demo)
- Before stakeholder meetings or calls
- As agenda for brief check-in calls

## Format (7 Sections)
1. What I built this week
2. What proof assets are live
3. What I posted
4. What leads I contacted
5. What I learned
6. What I'm doing next
7. Where support would help

## Tone Rules
- Calm, factual, proof-led — never desperate
- Show what shipped — don't promise what might ship
- Be honest about challenges — don't pretend everything is perfect
- State specific next steps — not vague "working on things"
- Ask for specific support — not general "help me out"
- Quantify where possible (counts, URLs, metrics)

## Quality Check
- [ ] Every section has at least one item
- [ ] Honest — empty sections say "nothing this week"
- [ ] Specific next steps listed
- [ ] Support ask is clear and specific
- [ ] No begging, no desperation, no oversharing
- [ ] Ready to send as Notion page, message, or call agenda
