# Localization Adapter

## Purpose
Adapt existing ad campaigns for African markets. Translate beyond words — adapt language, rhythm, music, pacing, visuals, and cultural context for specific countries or regions.

## When to Use
- A global brand needs ad adaptation for Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa, Ghana, or pan-African audiences
- An ecommerce or fintech client is entering a new African market
- A streaming or gaming client needs culturally relevant ad variants
- Building localization case studies for portfolio

## Inputs
- Original ad creative (script, visual direction, sound direction)
- Target market (country or region)
- Target audience persona for that market
- Platform for distribution

## Process
1. **Language Adaptation:** Translate with local idioms, not literal translation. Adjust tone.
2. **Rhythm & Music:** Replace soundtrack with locally resonant music. Adjust pacing.
3. **Visual Adaptation:** Replace culturally mismatched visuals. Adjust color palettes.
4. **Platform Adaptation:** Optimize for local dominant platforms. Adjust CTA flow.

## Output Per Market
1. Adapted script
2. Visual direction brief (what to change)
3. Sound direction brief (replacement music/sounds)
4. Platform-specific format guide
5. Optional: Kie-generated still with adapted visual direction
6. Optional: Suno-generated sound pack per market

## Rules
- [ ] Localization goes beyond translation (cultural, not just linguistic)
- [ ] Sound adaptation considers local music preferences
- [ ] Visual adaptation considers local talent and aesthetics
- [ ] No stereotyping or generic "African" visuals
- [ ] Each market gets a distinct adaptation, not a template
- [ ] Platform recommendations are based on local usage data, not assumptions
